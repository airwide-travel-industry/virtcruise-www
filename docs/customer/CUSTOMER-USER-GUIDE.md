## Guide overview

### Purpose

This guide helps you discover holidays, request and review a quote, make a booking, follow payment activity, and manage your VirtCruise account.

### Audience

This guide is for travellers. You do not need technical knowledge or previous experience with VirtCruise.

### Scope

This guide covers customer features available in product version 0.7.0 and customer-facing work accepted during Sprint 3.7. It explains the currently released Manual Finance Mode. Features labelled **Future functionality** are not currently available and are included only to prevent confusion.

It does not explain internal consultant, Finance, administration, content, or support procedures. Those roles use separate manuals.

### Before you begin

You need:

- a current email address that you can open;
- a supported web browser with cookies enabled;
- the right to accept the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy; and
- your quote, booking, invoice, or receipt reference when asking for help about a specific journey.

### Your journey through VirtCruise

```text
Register → Verify your email → Sign in → Browse packages
         → Request a quote → Accept a quote and create a booking
         → Follow Finance payment instructions → Booking confirmation
```

The steps do not all happen immediately. A quote is an offer for you to review. A booking is created when you accept an eligible quote. Finance must verify cleared funds before VirtCruise records payment or confirms a booking where payment is required.

### How to use this guide

Exact website labels appear in **bold**. Status values appear as `STATUS`. Each chapter explains the purpose, steps, expected result, helpful tips, warnings, common mistakes, and related chapters. Screenshots are marked as controlled placeholders until approved images can be captured with fictional data.

## 1. Welcome

### Purpose

Understand what you can do in VirtCruise and the normal path from account creation to a confirmed booking.

### Follow the customer journey

1. Select **Register** and create an account.
2. Open the verification message sent to your email address and follow its instructions.
3. Select **Sign In** and enter your account details.
4. Browse **Packages** and **Featured Tours**.
5. Request a quote for the holiday or services that interest you.
6. Review the quote in **My Quotes**.
7. Select **Accept quote** when the quote is eligible and you are ready to proceed.
8. Review the new booking in **My Bookings**.
9. Follow the payment instructions supplied directly by Finance.
10. Check the Financial Portal for recorded payment activity and receipts.
11. Wait for VirtCruise to confirm the booking after all required checks are complete.

**Expected result:** You know which areas of the website support each stage of your travel planning.

**If this does not work:** Go to [Contacting VirtCruise](#19-contacting-virtcruise) and provide the page name, time of the problem, and any safe on-screen reference.

## 3. Creating your account

### Purpose

Create a secure VirtCruise account and verify your email address.

### Register

**Prerequisites:** An email address you can open, and time to read the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

1. Select **Register**.
2. Enter your **First name** and **Last name**.
3. Enter your **Email address**.
4. Enter a password of at least 12 characters in **Password**.
5. Enter the same password in **Confirm password**.
6. Review the password-strength message and strengthen the password if requested.
7. Read the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
8. Select both acceptance boxes only if you agree.
9. Select **Create account**.
10. When the confirmation appears, open your email and follow the verification instructions.

**Expected result:** The website displays **Check your email to verify your account.** After you use a valid verification link, your account is ready for sign-in.

**If this does not work:** Check the form for a field-specific message. Correct the highlighted information and submit once more. If no verification message arrives, check spam or junk mail and confirm that you entered the correct address before contacting Support.

### Verify your email

1. Open the newest VirtCruise verification message.
2. Follow the verification link in that message.
3. Wait for the verification result.
4. Select the route back to sign-in when offered.

**Expected result:** VirtCruise confirms that your email has been verified, and you can sign in.

## 4. Signing in

### Purpose

Open your protected customer account and recover access safely when needed.

### Sign in with email and password

1. Select **Sign In**.
2. Enter your **Email address**.
3. Enter your **Password**.
4. Select **Remember me** only on a private device if you want the website to remember your email address.
5. Select **Sign in**.

You may also see **Continue with Google** and **Continue with Facebook**. Use one of these only when it is the same sign-in method associated with your VirtCruise account.

**Expected result:** Your account opens, or you return to the protected page you originally requested.

**If this does not work:** Check your email address and password carefully. If needed, use **Forgot password?**. Do not repeatedly guess a password.

### Reset a forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your **Email address**.
3. Select **Send reset instructions**.
4. Check your email. For privacy, the website uses the same general confirmation whether or not an eligible account is found.
5. Open the newest reset message and follow its link.
6. Enter a strong **New password** and confirm it.
7. Complete the reset, then return to **Sign In**.

**Expected result:** Your new password is accepted and can be used to sign in.

### Sign out

1. Open your account menu or the portal navigation.
2. Select **Logout**.
3. Confirm that a protected account page is no longer visible.

## 8. Requesting a quote

### Purpose

Send VirtCruise the information needed to prepare a travel quote, then review the resulting quote in your account.

### Submit a request

1. Browse to a package or service that interests you.
2. Select its quote or enquiry action.
3. Enter the requested trip information, such as destination, travel dates, and traveller details.
4. Add the requested flights, accommodation, transfers, visa services, activities, insurance, or special requests that apply.
5. Review every entry, especially names, dates, contact details, and the number of travellers.
6. Submit the request once.
7. Keep the confirmation or reference displayed by the website.

The available form can vary according to the package or services selected. Complete only information that is accurate and relevant.

**Expected result:** VirtCruise confirms receipt of the request. When a customer-visible quote is prepared, it appears under **My Quotes**.

**If this does not work:** Correct any field messages. If the result is uncertain, check **My Quotes** before submitting again. Contact Support if you cannot determine whether the request arrived.

### Review a quote

1. Sign in and select **My Quotes**.
2. Use **Search quotes**, **Status**, or **Sort by** to find the quote.
3. Select **View details**.
4. Review the itinerary, travellers, services, special requests, notes, status, and estimated quote value.
5. Use **Print quote** if you need a browser printout.
6. Do not proceed until the details are correct and you understand the conditions.

**Expected result:** You can review the complete customer-visible quote. The value is labelled as subject to availability and final confirmation.

> **Future functionality:** Downloadable itineraries, secure quote conversations, and customer quote-update requests are not currently available. Use [Contacting VirtCruise](#19-contacting-virtcruise) for assistance.

## 9. Making a booking

### Purpose

Create a booking from an eligible quote and understand what happens next.

### Accept a quote and create a booking

**Prerequisites:** You are signed in, the quote belongs to your account, and the quote displays **Accept quote**.

1. Select **My Quotes**.
2. Open the quote with **View details**.
3. Review the quote number, destination, dates, travellers, services, notes, value, and status.
4. Confirm that you want to proceed under the applicable terms.
5. Select **Accept quote** once.
6. Wait while VirtCruise creates the booking. Do not refresh or select the control again.
7. Review the booking details page that opens.
8. Keep the displayed booking reference.

**Expected result:** One booking is created and opens in **Booking details**. The booking also appears in **My Bookings**.

**If this does not work:** Check whether the booking already appears in **My Bookings**. If it does not, return to the quote and review its current status. Contact Support with the quote number if the result remains unclear.

### Review or accept a booking

Some bookings may show **Accept booking** when their status is `PENDING_CUSTOMER_ACCEPTANCE`.

1. Open **My Bookings**.
2. Select **View details** for the correct booking.
3. Review the booking and payment summary.
4. If **Accept booking** is displayed and you agree, select it once.
5. Wait for the updated booking details.

### Cancel a booking online

1. Open the booking details.
2. Enter a clear **Cancellation reason**.
3. Select **Cancel booking**.
4. Read the confirmation message carefully.
5. Confirm only if you intend to record the cancellation request.

**Expected result:** VirtCruise records the cancellation request and updates the booking when successful.

> **Warning:** The website warns that an online cancellation request cannot be undone online. Cancellation conditions and financial consequences may apply. Contact VirtCruise before acting if you are unsure.

## 10. Booking status

### Purpose

Understand the status displayed for your booking and identify the next safe action.

### Check a booking status

1. Sign in and select **My Bookings**.
2. Search by destination or booking reference if needed.
3. Choose a value under **Status** to narrow the list.
4. Select **View details** for the correct booking.
5. Review the status, booking timeline, travellers, services, and payment summary.
6. Check **Outstanding balance** and the payment status without assuming that either value changes immediately after a transfer.

**Expected result:** The current customer-visible status and booking information appear for the selected booking.

### Status guide

| Status | What it means for you | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `PENDING_CUSTOMER_ACCEPTANCE` | Your decision is required on the booking page. | Review all details and select **Accept booking** only if you agree. |
| `ACCEPTED` | The booking has been accepted and arrangements can progress. | Follow any consultant or Finance instructions. |
| `DEPOSIT_PENDING` | A required deposit has not yet been verified as received. | Follow Finance instructions and keep your booking reference. |
| `DEPOSIT_RECEIVED` | The required deposit is recorded as received. | Review the booking and wait for the next update. |
| `CONFIRMED` | VirtCruise has confirmed the booking. | Review **My Trips** and keep the confirmation. |
| `DOCUMENTS_PENDING` | Travel documents are still being prepared or checked. | Follow any direct request from VirtCruise. |
| `READY_TO_TRAVEL` | Current arrangements are ready for the travel stage. | Review your itinerary and direct communications. |
| `IN_PROGRESS` | The trip is under way. | Keep your itinerary and contact details available. |
| `COMPLETED` | The recorded trip is complete. | Retain receipts and travel records as needed. |
| `CANCELLED` | The booking is cancelled. | Contact VirtCruise if you need clarification about consequences. |
| `REFUNDED` | The booking record shows a completed refund outcome. | Compare the Financial Portal and contact Finance about discrepancies. |

Status labels can reflect the details of your journey. When a status conflicts with a direct communication, contact VirtCruise before taking financial or travel action.

## 12. Financial Portal

### Purpose

Review invoices, deposits, payments, receipts, and refunds recorded for your travel.

### Open your financial overview

1. Sign in.
2. Select **Finances**.
3. Review **Financial Overview**.
4. Check the outstanding balance, open invoices, next deposit due, and recent payment or receipt activity shown for the selected currency account.
5. Select **Invoices**, **Payments**, **Receipts**, or **Refunds** for the relevant history.

**Expected result:** You see only customer-safe financial records associated with your account.

### Review invoices and deposits

1. From **Financial Overview**, select **Invoices**.
2. Use **Search invoices** or the status filter to find a record on the current page.
3. Review invoice total, amount paid, outstanding balance, currency, status, and booking reference.
4. Select **View invoice** for line items and totals.
5. Use **Print invoice view** if you need a browser printout.
6. Review the separate deposit schedule for requested, received, and remaining amounts.

**Expected result:** The selected invoice appears as a read-only record. Amounts always include a currency and currencies are not combined.

### Review payments or refunds

1. Select **Payments** or **Refunds**.
2. Search by the available reference on the current page.
3. Filter by status when useful.
4. Review the amount, currency, booking reference where available, and current status.

**Expected result:** Recorded financial history appears. A missing record is not displayed as a zero balance.

> **Important:** Financial Portal pages do not provide an online payment control. They show confirmed financial activity and amounts recorded by VirtCruise.

## 13. Bank transfer payments

### Purpose

Pay safely by bank transfer using the currently released Finance-led process.

### Pay by bank transfer

**Prerequisites:** A booking or invoice that requires payment, access to your own banking service, and payment instructions supplied directly by authorised VirtCruise Finance staff.

1. Sign in and review the correct booking and invoice reference.
2. Select **Pay by Bank Transfer**.
3. Read the Manual Finance message and note the customer-safe invoice reference and currency shown.
4. Contact Finance using the contact route displayed on the page.
5. Ask Finance for the correct payment instructions for that invoice and currency.
6. Independently check that the message and payment details came through the approved Finance route.
7. Make the transfer through your bank, using the exact reference and amount instructed by Finance.
8. Keep your bank confirmation privately.
9. Wait while Finance checks that cleared funds have arrived and match your booking.
10. Review **Payments**, **Receipts**, and **My Bookings** for recorded updates.

**Expected result:** Finance verifies cleared funds, records the eligible payment, and progresses the booking. The booking remains pending until verification is complete.

**If this does not work:** Do not send a second transfer. Contact Finance with the booking or invoice reference, currency, amount, transfer date and time, and your bank's safe transaction reference. Do not send a password, security code, or full online-banking statement.

### Why no bank account is displayed

VirtCruise currently operates Manual Finance Mode. The website deliberately does not display a destination bank name, account number, branch code, or international transfer code. Finance supplies the instructions directly so the correct account and currency can be checked for your booking.

> **Warning:** Never transfer money to bank details taken from a search result, an old message, a social-media message, or a third party. Confirm any unexpected change using an approved VirtCruise contact route before paying.

### Future functionality

Self-service bank instructions, creation of a bank transfer review case, and proof upload are not available in Manual Finance Mode. Do not look for or rely on those controls. If a later approved release enables them, this guide must be reviewed before those steps are used.

## 14. Manual Finance Mode

### Purpose

Understand the released payment experience and why a Finance hand-off is required.

### What you see

When Manual Finance Mode is active, **Pay by Bank Transfer** remains available. The page displays:

- a message to contact Finance for payment instructions;
- approved Finance contact information supplied by VirtCruise;
- the invoice reference and currency associated with your record; and
- a control to copy the reference.

The page does not display bank account details. It does not offer automated proof submission, a review-case form, or self-service transfer tracking.

### Complete the Finance hand-off

1. Open **Pay by Bank Transfer**.
2. Confirm the invoice reference and currency.
3. Select **Copy reference** if available.
4. Contact Finance using the information on that page.
5. Provide the reference and currency, then follow the verified instructions Finance sends you.
6. After transferring, wait for Finance to verify cleared funds independently.
7. Check **Financial Overview** for a recorded payment and receipt.
8. Check **My Bookings** for the resulting booking status.

**Expected result:** Your payment and booking change only after Finance has verified and recorded the funds.

> **Important:** Sending money, sending a message, or providing a bank confirmation does not by itself mean that VirtCruise has recorded payment.

### Why the booking remains pending

A bank transfer can take time to reach the correct account and must be matched to the right invoice. Finance checks the currency, amount, reference, and cleared funds. Until that check succeeds, no payment is recorded and the booking remains at its current pending stage.

## 17. Frequently asked questions

### Purpose

Find concise answers to common account, package, quote, booking, payment, receipt, and Dashboard questions.

### Registration and email verification

1. **What do I need to register?**  
   You need your first and last name, an email address you can open, a strong password, and acceptance of the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

2. **How long must my password be?**  
   Use at least 12 characters and follow the strength guidance shown on the form.

3. **Why will the registration form not submit?**  
   Check for missing names, an invalid email address, passwords that do not match, a weak password, or a policy acceptance box that is not selected.

4. **Why do I need to verify my email?**  
   Verification helps protect the account and confirms that you can receive messages at that address.

5. **My verification email has not arrived. What should I do?**  
   Check spam or junk mail, confirm the address you entered, allow normal delivery time, and use the newest message. Contact Support if it still does not arrive.

6. **Can I register more than once with the same email address?**  
   Use your existing account. If you cannot access it, reset the password or contact Support instead of creating duplicates.

### Sign-in and password reset

7. **Why can I not sign in?**  
   Check that the email is verified and that the email address and password are correct. Use **Forgot password?** if necessary.

8. **What does Remember me do?**  
   It remembers your email address in that browser. Select it only on a private device.

9. **Can I sign in with Google or Facebook?**  
   Those options are shown on the sign-in page. Use the method associated with your account.

10. **How do I reset my password?**  
    Select **Forgot password?**, enter your email address, and follow the instructions in the newest reset email.

11. **Why does password reset use a general confirmation message?**  
    The message protects account privacy by not revealing whether a particular email address is registered.

12. **What if my reset link does not work?**  
    Request a new reset message and use the newest link. If it still fails, contact Support without forwarding the link.

13. **How do I sign out?**  
    Open the account or portal menu and select **Logout**.

### Packages and featured tours

14. **Where can I see all holiday packages?**  
    Open **Packages** and select **Explore All Packages**.

15. **What is a Featured Tour?**  
    It is a holiday package highlighted by VirtCruise to help you discover travel ideas.

16. **Does a displayed package guarantee availability?**  
    No. Availability and final arrangements must be confirmed through the quote and booking process.

17. **Is the displayed package price my final price?**  
    Not necessarily. Review your personalised quote, which remains subject to availability and final confirmation.

18. **Can I ask for a change to a package?**  
    Include relevant needs in the quote request or contact VirtCruise. Secure quote conversations and online update requests are future functionality.

### Quotes

19. **What is the difference between a request and a quote?**  
    A request gives VirtCruise your needs. A quote is the customer-visible offer prepared from those needs.

20. **Where do I find my quotes?**  
    Sign in and select **My Quotes**.

21. **How can I find a particular quote?**  
    Search by quote number, destination, or package name, and use the status and sort controls.

22. **Is a quote a booking?**  
    No. An eligible quote becomes a booking only after you select **Accept quote** and booking creation succeeds.

23. **Can I download my itinerary from a quote?**  
    Not currently. Downloadable itinerary generation is future functionality. You can use **Print quote** for the current browser view.

24. **Why can I not see Accept quote?**  
    The control appears only for an eligible quote in the accepted state. Review its status or contact VirtCruise.

### Bookings and Dashboard

25. **How do I make a booking?**  
    Open an eligible quote, review it carefully, and select **Accept quote** once.

26. **Where is my new booking?**  
    It should open after quote acceptance and appear under **My Bookings**.

27. **Does accepting a quote confirm my travel?**  
    No. It creates the booking. Payment and operational checks may still be required before status becomes `CONFIRMED`.

28. **How do I check booking status?**  
    Open **My Bookings**, find the reference, and select **View details**.

29. **Why is my booking still pending after I transferred money?**  
    Finance must first verify cleared funds and match them to your invoice. Until then, payment is not recorded and the booking remains pending.

30. **Can I cancel a booking online?**  
    If the booking page offers **Cancel booking**, enter a reason and confirm carefully. The request cannot be undone online, and conditions may apply.

31. **What does the Customer Dashboard show?**  
    It summarises trips, quotes, saved travellers, notifications, security, recent activity, and common actions.

32. **Why is a Dashboard section empty?**  
    A new account or a stage with no matching records can have an empty state. Open the detailed page to check.

33. **Can I print a booking?**  
    Yes. Open its details and use **Print booking**.

### Payments, Manual Finance, and receipts

34. **Can I pay online in VirtCruise?**  
    No online payment control is currently released. Finance supplies payment instructions directly in Manual Finance Mode.

35. **Why are no bank account details displayed?**  
    Manual Finance Mode deliberately directs you to Finance so the correct instructions can be checked for your invoice and currency.

36. **How do I get bank transfer instructions?**

37. **Should I upload proof of payment?**

38. **Does my bank confirmation mean VirtCruise has received payment?**

39. **Where can I see my outstanding balance?**

40. **Where can I see payments recorded by VirtCruise?**  
    Open **Finances** > **Payments**.

41. **When will I receive a receipt?**  
    An eligible receipt appears after Finance has verified and recorded the payment.

42. **Can I download a receipt PDF?**  
    Not currently. PDF receipt download is future functionality.

43. **What is the difference between an invoice and a receipt?**  
    An invoice shows an amount charged or due. A receipt records an eligible payment that VirtCruise has received and recorded.

44. **Can I combine balances in different currencies?**  
    No. Review each currency separately; the portal does not calculate exchange conversions or a combined total.

45. **Who should I contact about a missing payment or receipt?**  
    Contact Finance with safe references, the currency, amount, and transfer date. Never send a password or security code.

    Expanded profile and preference information currently uses browser-only storage and may not appear elsewhere.

47. **Can I save passport documents?**  
    Passport document upload is future functionality. Do not send passport images through an unapproved contact route.

48. **Where do quote and booking messages appear?**  
    Select **Notifications**. Important transactional messages may also arrive by email.

49. **Are push notifications available?**  
    No. Browser and mobile push notifications are planned for a future release.

50. **What information should I give Support?**  
    Give the page name, date and time, quote or booking reference if relevant, and any safe Support details reference. Never give your password or full financial details.

## 20. Glossary

### Purpose

Understand the words used throughout the customer journey.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Booking | The record created when you accept an eligible quote and booking creation succeeds. It can still require payment and operational checks before confirmation. |
| Booking Confirmation | The outcome that tells you VirtCruise has confirmed the booking after required checks. Quote acceptance or sending a bank transfer is not by itself confirmation. |
| Booking reference | The customer-safe reference used to find and discuss a booking. |
| Customer Dashboard | The signed-in starting page that summarises your quotes, trips, travellers, notifications, security, and recent activity. |
| Deposit | A required part-payment associated with a booking. The Financial Portal separates the amount requested, received, and remaining. |
| Featured Tour | A holiday package highlighted by VirtCruise to help travellers discover ideas. Featured does not guarantee availability or a discount. |
| Finance | The authorised VirtCruise team that supplies payment instructions and verifies and records eligible payments. |
| Financial Portal | The read-only customer area for financial overview, invoices, deposits, payments, receipts, and refunds. It does not currently take online payments. |
| Invoice | A financial record showing charges, amounts paid, and the outstanding balance for a booking. It is not proof of payment. |
| Manual Finance | The currently released mode in which Finance gives bank transfer instructions directly and verifies cleared funds before recording payment. No bank account details or proof-upload controls are displayed on the website. |
| Notification | A customer portal message about quote, booking, or travel activity. Current read state is browser-only. |
| Outstanding balance | The amount the current record shows as not yet paid or allocated, stated in a particular currency. |
| Package | A published holiday idea with destination and service information. It is not a reservation. |
| Payment | Money that Finance has verified and recorded against the relevant customer financial record. Sending a transfer does not immediately create this record. |
| Quote | A customer-visible offer describing proposed travel arrangements and estimated value. It is subject to availability and final confirmation. |
| Receipt | A record issued after an eligible payment has been verified and recorded. A bank transfer confirmation is not a VirtCruise receipt. |
| Refund | Money recorded as being returned in relation to an eligible payment or booking, with a status visible in the Financial Portal. |
| Traveller | A customer or person included in travel planning. |

1. Identify whether the record is a quote, booking, invoice, payment, or receipt.
2. Copy the reference from that record.
3. State the record type when contacting VirtCruise.
4. Check the amount and currency on financial records.
