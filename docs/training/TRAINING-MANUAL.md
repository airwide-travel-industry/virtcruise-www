## 1. Introduction

This manual defines the official VirtCruise staff training programme for onboarding, role transition, refresher training, supervisor assessment, and internal certification. It teaches people how to find and apply approved guidance under realistic pressure; it does not duplicate the full procedures.

> **Important:** This programme does not replace DOC-002, DOC-003, DOC-004, DOC-005, DOC-006, DOC-007, DOC-008, or DOC-010. Learners must use the applicable manual for the procedure and DOC-007 for every status meaning and legal transition. Training access, fictional exercises, or certification never grant production authority.

No frontend, backend, application, implementation, deployment, production, configuration, or test change is included.

### Training audiences

Customer Support, Consultants, Finance, Content Editors, Operations, Administrators, Supervisors, and Managers complete the common core and their assigned role pathway. Trainers must not expose a learner to live customer, payment, proof, credential, or production data.

### How to use the programme

1. Assign the common core and role pathway using section 4.
2. Complete required reading before facilitated practice.
3. Use only the fictional scenarios and sandbox/training environment authorised by policy.
4. Record observed competence, not attendance alone.
5. Administer the role assessment without coaching.
6. Resolve gaps, reassess failed objectives, and obtain supervisor sign-off.
7. Issue certification only under [Training Certification](TRAINING-CERTIFICATION.md).

## 2. Training objectives

The programme enables learners to:

- navigate DOC-002–DOC-008 and select the owning source quickly;
- distinguish customer, quote, booking, review, proof, Financial, notification, and content lifecycles;
- verify identity, ownership, permission, current state, and required evidence before action;
- complete frequent role tasks through approved workflows;
- protect confidential information and follow least privilege;
- communicate accurate, empathetic, customer-safe outcomes;
- recognise exceptions, stop unsafe action, and escalate to the accountable owner;
- demonstrate competence through knowledge, scenario, lab, and checklist assessment; and
- maintain competence through supervised practice and refresher training.

## 4. Training programme overview

### Programme paths

| Path | Required modules | Guided practice | Assessment | Typical elapsed learning time |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| New employee | Orientation, common core, assigned role, exercises/labs | Supervised | Common + role | 12–18 hours plus workplace observation |
| Role transition | Common-core delta, new role, exercises/labs | Supervised in new scope | New role + observed practice | 6–12 hours |
| Refresher | Change/risk topics and selected scenarios | Targeted | Knowledge check or role reassessment | 2–4 hours |
| Supervisor | Common core, own operational role, assessment/certification | Facilitation practice | Supervisor assessment | 6–8 hours plus observation |
| Manager | Governance, risk, certification, escalation | Tabletop | Manager assessment | 3–5 hours |

Times are planning estimates, not service levels. Trainers adjust for evidence-based needs without removing completion criteria.

### Delivery sequence

```text
Enrol → Read Quick Start → Common core → Role module → Scenarios
→ Guided labs → Knowledge check → Role assessment → Supervisor observation
→ Gap closure / reassessment → Certification → Scheduled refresher
```

**Figure 1 — Official training pathway.** Failed evidence returns only to the affected objective; the original attempt remains recorded.

### Training environment rules

- Use fictional records clearly marked `TRAINING`, such as customer **Amina Dube**, quote `VC-Q-TRAIN-1042`, booking `VC-B-TRAIN-2042`, invoice `VC-I-TRAIN-3042`, and case `VC-R-TRAIN-4042`.
- Use `example.com` addresses and non-routable/sample contact values; never reuse production credentials, tokens, bank details, proof, or personal data.
- A trainer demonstrates risky decisions before the learner practises.
- Labs that simulate approval, rejection, publication, restoration, permission changes, notification recovery, or incident action must run in an authorised non-production environment or as tabletop exercises.
- Record scores and observation evidence in the approved learning record, not in operational/customer notes.

### Module contract

Every module below lists learning objectives, estimated duration, required reading, practical exercises, knowledge check, and completion criteria. “Discuss” is not sufficient evidence where the criterion requires the learner to perform or decide.

## 5. Customer module

### Learning objectives

Explain the complete customer route; demonstrate account creation/verification/sign-in guidance; browse packages and request a quote; distinguish acceptance, booking, Manual Finance, Payment, Receipt, and Booking Confirmation; and locate the appropriate support route.

### Estimated duration

60 minutes reading/demonstration plus 45 minutes practice.

### Required reading

[DOC-002 Customer User Guide](../customer/CUSTOMER-USER-GUIDE.md), [Customer Quick Start](../quickstart/CUSTOMER-QUICK-START.md), and DOC-007 sections 4–6, 12–14, and 21.

### Practical exercises

Complete Exercises EX-01 and EX-02 and Lab LAB-01 in [Training Exercises](TRAINING-EXERCISES.md). Explain why a submitted quote, transfer, proof, Receipt, and notification do not independently confirm a booking.

### Knowledge check

Answer KC-CU-01–KC-CU-05 in Appendix B without notes. Correct any unsafe statement aloud.

### Completion criteria

At least 4/5 knowledge answers correct; complete the fictional customer route without accessing another customer, duplicating submission, exposing secrets, or promising confirmation; identify the correct manual for one exception.

## 6. Support module

### Learning objectives

Verify customers proportionately; classify the owning lifecycle; guide password reset safely; explain Manual Finance; document minimum facts; handle complaints; recognise incidents; and escalate while retaining customer communication ownership.

### Estimated duration

90 minutes instruction plus 90 minutes practice.

### Required reading

[DOC-005 Support Playbook](../support/CUSTOMER-SUPPORT-PLAYBOOK.md), [Support Quick Start](../quickstart/SUPPORT-QUICK-START.md), DOC-002 customer procedures, DOC-003 escalation, and DOC-007 customer/internal boundaries.

### Practical exercises

Complete EX-01, EX-03, EX-07, EX-10, and LAB-08. Role-play one email, telephone, and chat response using the same verified facts.

### Knowledge check

Answer KC-SU-01–KC-SU-05 and have a peer identify any promise, speculation, excess data, or unclear ownership.

### Completion criteria

At least 4/5 knowledge answers correct; 100% on password/secret and privacy critical items; produce a complete safe case note and acknowledged escalation; communicate empathy without promising outcome or time.

## 7. Operations module

### Learning objectives

Complete daily controls; distinguish state from queue condition; compare review, Financial, booking, notification, and projection records; coordinate owners; manage service incidents; and complete end-of-day handover.

### Estimated duration

120 minutes instruction plus 120 minutes practice.

### Required reading

[DOC-003 Operations Manual](../operations/BACK-OFFICE-OPERATIONS-MANUAL.md), [Operations Quick Start](../quickstart/OPERATIONS-QUICK-START.md), DOC-004 exception boundaries, DOC-005 communications, and DOC-007.

### Practical exercises

Complete EX-07 and EX-10 plus LAB-02, LAB-04, and LAB-08. Facilitate a tabletop where Financial processing completed but the booking projection did not.

### Knowledge check

Answer KC-OP-01–KC-OP-05 and explain the safe response to retry, uncertainty, suppression, stale claim, and manual intervention.

### Completion criteria

At least 4/5 knowledge answers correct; complete morning and end-of-day checklists; assign correct owners to every exercise; make no direct record edit or blind replay; produce an incident handover another operator can continue.

## 8. Finance module

### Learning objectives

Apply segregation of duties; handle Manual Finance; triage and assign review work; open only current accepted/clean proof; verify cleared funds independently; approve or reject once; recognise replacement limitations; and reconcile Payment, allocation, Receipt, Ledger, booking, and notification outcomes.

### Estimated duration

180 minutes instruction plus 180 minutes guided practice and observation.

### Required reading

[DOC-004 Finance SOP](../finance/FINANCE-STANDARD-OPERATING-PROCEDURES.md), DOC-003 sections 11–23, [Finance Quick Start](../quickstart/FINANCE-QUICK-START.md), and DOC-007 sections 7–14 and 18–20.

### Practical exercises

Complete EX-03–EX-06 and LAB-03–LAB-05. One approval and one rejection must be tabletop or non-production, using fictional proof metadata and an independent fictional clearing record.

### Knowledge check

Answer KC-FI-01–KC-FI-05. The trainer challenges one decision with a mismatch and one timeout/conflict.

### Completion criteria

At least 4/5 knowledge answers correct and 100% on proof/cleared-funds, duplicate, and direct-edit critical items; complete both decision checklists; reconcile all downstream objects separately; stop safely on conflict.

## 9. Content management module

### Learning objectives

Create and edit only a `DRAFT`; preserve version history; validate structured content, pricing, media, accessibility, and search engine optimisation (SEO); submit for independent review; simulate approve/reject; schedule/publish; retire; and restore by deriving a new version.

### Estimated duration

150 minutes instruction plus 150 minutes practice.

### Required reading

[DOC-006 Content Studio User Guide](../content/CONTENT-STUDIO-USER-GUIDE.md), [Content Editor Quick Start](../quickstart/CONTENT-EDITOR-QUICK-START.md), DOC-007 sections 15–16, and the DOC-006 publishing checklist.

### Practical exercises

Complete EX-08, EX-09, LAB-06, and LAB-07. The Content Studio UI is planned, so use an accepted training environment or tabletop version records rather than claiming an unreleased screen.

### Knowledge check

Answer KC-CE-01–KC-CE-05 and identify every immutable version in a sample history.

### Completion criteria

At least 4/5 knowledge answers correct; no in-place edit of a submitted/rejected/retired version; all pricing/media/accessibility checks complete; independent approval preserved; public projection verified after simulated publication.

## 10. Administrator module

### Learning objectives

Describe system domains and role boundaries; validate access requests; apply least privilege; coordinate authentication recovery; diagnose denied actions; preserve audit evidence; and separate Administrator capability from Finance, Operations, Content, or business authority.

### Estimated duration

90 minutes instruction plus 90 minutes tabletop practice.

### Required reading

DOC-003 role matrix and access/incident sections, [Administrator Quick Start](../quickstart/ADMINISTRATOR-QUICK-START.md), DOC-004 segregation, DOC-005 recovery/security, and DOC-007.

### Practical exercises

Complete EX-01, EX-10, and LAB-09. Review one legitimate least-privilege request and reject one informal overbroad request with a safe explanation.

### Knowledge check

Answer KC-AD-01–KC-AD-05 and state why `ROLE_ADMIN` or a visible control does not grant business authority.

### Completion criteria

At least 4/5 knowledge answers correct and 100% on credentials, self-approval, and Finance-authority critical items; record approval/evidence without secrets; route each domain decision correctly.

## 11. Security awareness

### Learning objectives

Protect confidentiality; use least privilege; recognise phishing, social engineering, suspicious proof, credential compromise, fraud indicators, and unsafe channels; preserve evidence; and escalate without conducting an amateur investigation.

### Estimated duration

75 minutes instruction plus 45 minutes scenario practice.

### Required reading

DOC-003 security/fraud sections, DOC-004 fraud/proof handling, DOC-005 security, applicable DOC-008 guide, and approved Security policy.

### Practical exercises

Classify five fictional messages/files; demonstrate safe refusal of a password/one-time-code request; complete EX-11 in [Training Exercises](TRAINING-EXERCISES.md).

### Knowledge check

Answer KC-SE-01–KC-SE-05. Explain the difference between preserving facts and investigating.

### Completion criteria

5/5 critical knowledge answers correct; no suspicious link/file opened; correct urgent route selected; minimum evidence preserved without forwarding harmful or confidential content.

## 12. Privacy

### Learning objectives

Use minimum necessary data; verify identity and ownership; prevent cross-customer exposure; use approved channels; handle proof/private records safely; recognise privacy incidents; and stop further disclosure.

### Estimated duration

60 minutes instruction plus 45 minutes practice.

### Required reading

DOC-002 privacy/security, DOC-003 confidentiality, DOC-004 privacy, DOC-005 privacy, DOC-006 privacy/media controls, and approved Privacy policy.

### Practical exercises

Redact an over-collected case note, classify five data elements, and complete EX-12. Use fictional data only.

### Knowledge check

Answer KC-PR-01–KC-PR-05 and identify the minimum facts for a Finance versus Security escalation.

### Completion criteria

5/5 critical knowledge answers correct; stop and report simulated cross-customer exposure immediately; produce a minimum-necessary handoff with no secrets, proof content, or unnecessary personal data.

## 13. Communication standards

### Learning objectives

Use professional language, empathy, plain English, bounded status explanations, clear ownership, and approved expectations across email, telephone, and chat; manage difficult conversations without blame, speculation, or unsupported promises.

### Estimated duration

75 minutes instruction plus 60 minutes role-play.

### Required reading

DOC-005 communication/complaint sections, DOC-003 customer communication boundaries, DOC-004 Finance communication, DOC-002 customer terminology, and DOC-007 customer-visible wording.

### Practical exercises

Rewrite five unsafe messages; deliver the same fictional update by email, phone, and chat; de-escalate an upset customer in EX-05.

### Knowledge check

Answer KC-CM-01–KC-CM-05. Peer review against facts, empathy, action, owner, expectation, privacy, and tone.

### Completion criteria

At least 4/5 knowledge answers correct; all critical privacy/promise items correct; communication states what is known and next without internal detail; difficult conversation remains respectful and controlled.

## 14. Customer service excellence

### Learning objectives

Listen actively; recognise intent and impact; take ownership of communication; reduce customer effort; set honest expectations; handle complaints; close the loop; and learn from recurring issues.

### Estimated duration

60 minutes instruction plus 60 minutes practice.

### Required reading

DOC-005 principles, complaint handling, safe wording, and handover template; DOC-002 customer journey; assigned DOC-008 guide.

### Practical exercises

Run a three-person role-play (customer, staff, observer) for EX-02 and EX-10. Observer records behaviors, not personality judgments.

### Knowledge check

Answer KC-CS-01–KC-CS-05 and identify one way to reduce repeat contact without bypassing verification or workflow.

### Completion criteria

At least 4/5 correct; acknowledge impact, verify facts, provide next owner/action, avoid false assurance, document accurately, and confirm the customer understands the next step.

## 15. Scenario exercises

The authoritative exercise pack is [Training Exercises](TRAINING-EXERCISES.md). It contains twelve realistic scenarios covering forgotten password, booking status, payment under review, payment dispute, refund request, proof rejection, delayed notification, package update, publication mistake, system unavailability, security/fraud, and privacy exposure.

Facilitators must not reveal expected decisions until learners submit their response. Assess decisions against the owning manual, not preferred phrasing. A learner may ask for an approved policy or current state; recognising missing information is competence.

## 16. Practical labs

The exercise pack includes nine guided labs: locate customer/quote, locate booking and interpret lifecycle, review payment/review status, make a sample approval/rejection decision, reconcile downstream Financial records, prepare/publish a package, restore a previous version safely, build an escalation/handover, and assess access/permissions.

Labs use fictional data and either an authorised training environment or tabletop artifacts. A trainer must stop any attempt to use production data or execute an unauthorised mutation.

## 17. Knowledge checks

### Use and scoring

Module knowledge checks are formative. Learners answer before discussion, mark the source supporting each correction, and retain the corrected version in the training record. Passing a module check does not replace the role assessment.

### Knowledge-check bank

Appendix B provides 50 short checks (`KC-*`). Trainers may randomise wording but must preserve the decision being tested. Answers appear in Appendix C, separate from the learner-facing question bank.

Critical errors include exposing secrets/private data, approving without cleared-funds verification, opening unsafe proof, bypassing lifecycle/segregation, direct state editing, blind retry/resend, or failing to escalate active security/privacy exposure. Any critical error requires remediation regardless of aggregate score.

## 18. Role assessments

[Training Assessments](TRAINING-ASSESSMENTS.md) contains 112 questions across Customer Support, Consultant, Finance, Content Editor, Operations, Administrator, Supervisor, and Manager roles. Every role includes multiple choice, true/false, short answer, scenario analysis, operational decision, and checklist review. The answer key and scoring guidance are in a separate section after all learner questions.

Recommended pass level is 80% overall plus 100% of critical safety items. Finance, Security/Privacy, and Supervisor pathways also require observed practical competence. Failed items are remediated and reassessed with an equivalent alternate item; original scores remain in the record.

## 19. Certification

[Training Certification](TRAINING-CERTIFICATION.md) defines Foundation, Role Practitioner, Advanced/High-Risk Practitioner, and Supervisor/Assessor levels; prerequisites; evidence; approval; refresher intervals; suspension; role transition; and re-certification.

Certification confirms demonstrated training competence within approved role/permission boundaries. It is not production access, delegation of authority, employment approval, or permission to bypass supervision.

## 20. Supervisor sign-off

Printable sign-off forms are in Training Certification. The supervisor records employee, role, trainer, supervisor, dates, modules, assessment result, practical evidence, competency achieved, outstanding items, restrictions, refresher date, and signatures/approved electronic attestations.

A supervisor must withhold sign-off when evidence is missing, a critical error is unresolved, access/authority is unclear, or an assessment conflict exists. Business pressure is not a reason to certify.

**Finance:** 01 current proof `ACCEPTED`, scan `CLEAN`, allowed format, authorised viewer/user; 02 no; 03 assignment, eligible `UNDER_REVIEW`, exact match, independent cleared funds, duplicate/fraud/privacy checks, reason/attestation; 04 refresh and inspect authoritative outcome; 05 no, not in accepted backend path.

**Content:** 01 `DRAFT`; 02 version becomes immutable `IN_REVIEW`; 03 derive a new numbered `DRAFT`; 04 no, restoration is derivation; 05 separately authorised independent Approver.

**Administrator:** 01 no; 02 verified requester, business-owner approval, purpose, least privilege, duration/review, recorded reference; 03 no; 04 minimum access needed for task/time; 05 approved business/access owner.

**Security:** 01 keep closed/isolate and escalate through approved route; 02 passwords, tokens, one-time codes, reset links, private keys; 03 suspected compromise, malicious content, exposure, or increasing risk; 04 no; 05 record minimum facts/references without opening, copying, forwarding, or altering suspicious material.

**Privacy:** 01 only data necessary for the approved task; 02 stop disclosure, preserve facts, report immediately; 03 no; 04 verification of identity, ownership, permission, and need; 05 Security/Privacy owner.

**Communication:** 01 verified fact/current state, impact acknowledgement, next action, owner, approved expectation; 02 no; 03 internal comments, reviewer/audit/storage/message bodies/recipient lists/security detail; 04 specifically and without blame/speculation; 05 receiving owner acknowledges.

**Customer service:** 01 listening, clarifying, and reflecting the actual need/impact; 02 use correct source/reference and complete safe first-contact steps; 03 verify result and customer understanding/next update; 04 no; 05 identify documentation, process, product, or training improvement through approved review.

### Appendix D — Role checklist

- [ ] Role purpose, responsibilities, authority, exclusions, and escalation owners explained.
- [ ] Required manuals and Quick Start located without prompting.
- [ ] At least two routine tasks performed correctly.
- [ ] At least two exception decisions performed or stopped safely.
- [ ] Security/privacy critical behaviors demonstrated.
- [ ] Customer-safe communication and handover demonstrated.
- [ ] Role assessment and practical observation complete.

### Appendix E — Assessment checklist

- [ ] Identity and assessment version recorded; approved conditions established.
- [ ] Coaching/material rules stated; conflicts/accommodations recorded.
- [ ] All required item types administered.
- [ ] Score and critical items independently checked.
- [ ] Scenario/lab evidence attached by reference, not sensitive content.
- [ ] Gaps and remediation stated specifically.
- [ ] Reassessment uses equivalent alternate evidence and preserves first attempt.

### Appendix F — Certification checklist

- [ ] Required modules/readings/current changes complete.
- [ ] Assessment threshold and all critical items passed.
- [ ] Practical/workplace observation passed for role risk.
- [ ] Supervisor sign-off complete with restrictions/outstanding items.
- [ ] Certification level, role scope, issue/due dates, and evidence references recorded.
- [ ] Access provisioning remains a separate approved process.

### Appendix G — Supervisor checklist

- [ ] Observe decisions and controls, not speed or confidence alone.
- [ ] Use the published rubric consistently and disclose conflicts.
- [ ] Stop unsafe practice immediately and protect fictional/training boundaries.
- [ ] Give specific evidence-based feedback and allow remediation.
- [ ] Withhold sign-off for missing/critical evidence.
- [ ] Schedule refresher/re-certification and monitor change triggers.
