## 1. Administration and scoring

This bank contains 112 questions: 14 for each of eight roles. Each role includes four multiple-choice, two true/false, two short-answer, two scenario-analysis, two operational-decision, and two checklist-review items. Questions use fictional data and DOC-002–DOC-008.

Recommended pass: 80% overall for the assigned role (12/14 when whole-item scoring is used) and 100% of critical safety items marked **[Critical]**. Short/scenario/decision/checklist items score against all answer elements; the assessor may use 0 (unsafe/absent), 1 (partial), or 2 (complete), then normalise the role total. Any critical error requires remediation and equivalent reassessment regardless of aggregate score.

The answer key begins in section 10 after all learner questions. Do not provide it during a closed assessment. Record attempt/version, permitted materials, accommodations, assessor, result, critical findings, and evidence reference.
