## 1. Purpose

Define consistent internal certification, supervisor approval, refresher, re-certification, suspension, evidence, and recordkeeping for VirtCruise staff competence.

> **Important:** Certification is evidence of training competence within a named scope. It is not production access, a role grant, business delegation, policy approval, external accreditation, or permission to bypass supervision. This document does not replace DOC-002–DOC-008 or DOC-010.

## 2. Certification levels

| Level | Intended audience | Evidence required | Approval | Recommended validity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 — Foundation | All staff before role practice | Orientation/common core, security, privacy, communication, formative checks | Trainer | 12 months; refresh sooner on trigger |
| Level 2 — Role Practitioner | Support, Consultant, Content Editor, Operations, Administrator | Level 1, role reading/module, required scenarios/labs, ≥80% role assessment, all critical items, observed routine/exception work | Role Supervisor | 12 months |
| Level 3 — Advanced / High-Risk Practitioner | Finance and designated high-risk operational/content/access duties | Level 2-equivalent, advanced role labs, segregation/critical controls, ≥80% role assessment, all critical items, two satisfactory observations | Role Supervisor + functional lead | 6 months for Finance/high-risk scope |
| Level 4 — Supervisor / Assessor | Supervisors and approved assessors | Appropriate practitioner level, supervisor module/assessment, observed facilitation/scoring/sign-off, conflict/rubric competence | Functional lead + Learning and Development | 12 months |
| Manager endorsement | Managers making readiness/risk decisions | Manager assessment, governance briefing, certification/risk tabletop | Business Owner or delegate | 12 months |

Validity is a recommended governance maximum, not an employment promise. Approved policy may require a shorter interval. Change/risk triggers override the calendar.

## 3. Completion requirements

### Common requirements

- Current assigned DOC-002–DOC-008 reading completed as applicable.
- All required module completion criteria met.
- Required fictional scenarios and non-production/tabletop labs completed.
- Assigned assessment meets recommended 80% threshold.
- Every critical security, privacy, proof, cleared-funds, segregation, direct-edit, and retry item passes.
- Practical/workplace evidence shows both routine task and exception handling.
- Gaps are remediated and reassessed with equivalent evidence; original attempts retained.
- Supervisor confirms role/authority boundaries and records restrictions/outstanding items.

### Role evidence matrix

| Role | Minimum practical evidence before sign-off |
|---|---|
| Customer Support | Verification, password-reset guidance, booking/status explanation, safe case note, Finance/incident/privacy escalation |
| Consultant | Approved lookup, quote explanation, booking/Finance boundary, customer update, acknowledged handoff |
| Finance | Queue/assignment, safe proof decision, one approval and rejection simulation, conflict handling, full downstream reconciliation |
| Content Editor | Draft/version check, pricing/media/SEO/accessibility review, independent submission/review, publication and restore simulation |
| Operations | Morning/end checks, queue/state distinction, incident coordination, notification/projection exception, complete handover |
| Administrator | Access-request validation, least privilege, recovery coordination, Finance-authority refusal, audit-safe record |
| Supervisor | Objective observation, critical-error handling, fair scoring, remediation design, complete sign-off |
| Manager | Cohort risk analysis, policy-gap response, certification exception refusal/control, change-trigger plan |

## 4. Supervisor approval

The supervisor reviews the fixed training/assessment version, evidence references, critical items, observed behaviors, restrictions, conflicts, accommodations, and currency of procedure/policy. The supervisor may approve, approve with a narrow recorded restriction where policy allows, or withhold pending remediation.

The supervisor must withhold when:

- a critical objective failed or was not observed;
- evidence is live/unauthorised, incomplete, coached during a closed assessment, or cannot be traced;
- role authority, segregation, or access approval is unclear;
- the learner used direct edits, unsafe proof, secrets, excess personal data, blind replay/resend, or unsupported promises;
- material procedures changed after training; or
- the assessor has an unmanaged conflict.

Business urgency never converts incomplete evidence into competence.

## 5. Refresher intervals and triggers

| Population | Recommended scheduled refresher | Typical focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Finance/high-risk proof or Financial roles | Every 6 months | Cleared funds, proof safety, duplicates, reconciliation, lifecycle changes |
| Security/Privacy incident responders | Every 6 months | Threat/privacy scenarios, evidence, urgent routes |
| Support, Consultant, Content, Operations, Administrator | Every 12 months | Role changes, recurring errors, customer communication, exceptions |
| Supervisors/Assessors/Managers | Every 12 months | Rubric consistency, critical errors, governance, workforce risk |

Start an earlier review after product/manual/policy/law change; role transition; material incident or audit finding; critical operational error; long absence; repeated customer/support trend; access/scope change; assessment-bank revision; or supervisor concern.

Refresher evidence can be a focused reading delta, scenario, knowledge check, observation, or full reassessment according to risk. Attendance alone does not close a demonstrated competence gap.

## 6. Re-certification guidance

1. Start before the validity due date and confirm current role/scope/access separately.
2. Review changes since the prior certification, including DOC-007 and owning manuals.
3. Complete scheduled refresher plus affected scenarios/labs.
4. Administer a current equivalent assessment; do not reuse revealed answers as sole evidence.
5. Observe current practical performance proportionate to risk.
6. Review incidents, gaps, restrictions, absence, and prior remediation without assuming fault.
7. Record approve/withhold, new validity date, evidence, restrictions, and next trigger.

Expired certification must be treated under approved workforce/access policy; this document does not automatically revoke or grant system access. The manager ensures appropriate supervision and coordinates with access/business owners.

## 7. Suspension, withdrawal, and reinstatement

A functional lead may recommend temporary suspension of certified scope when a critical safety/privacy/security event, material procedure change, extended absence, or evidence-integrity concern creates unacceptable training risk. Follow employment, incident, access, and fairness policies; do not prejudge an investigation.

Withdrawal requires an accountable decision and reason. Reinstatement requires documented remediation, current equivalent assessment, practical evidence, and new supervisor approval. Preserve the original certificate and decision history.

## 8. Role transition and equivalence

Prior Foundation certification may transfer when current and relevant. The new role's responsibilities, manuals, practical skills, assessment, and observation never transfer automatically. The supervisor records each waived module and objective with evidence; silence is not equivalence.

Finance/high-risk transition requires Level 3 evidence even if the person previously held Administrator or Supervisor certification. Technical access does not establish business competence.

## 9. Certification records and privacy

Store only necessary employee identity, role/scope, versions, dates, scores/results, critical findings, evidence references, assessor/supervisor, restrictions, remediation, validity, and approvals in the approved learning record. Do not store customer/proof data, assessment secrets in learner-visible fields, medical detail beyond approved accommodation handling, credentials, or unrelated performance commentary.

Access is limited to the learner, authorised supervisor/manager, Learning and Development, Human Resources, audit/compliance, and other roles under approved policy. Retention and employee rights are policy-owned.

## 13. Certification governance checklist

- [ ] Current programme/manual/assessment versions used.
- [ ] Trainer, assessor, and supervisor authority/conflicts checked.
- [ ] Required reading, modules, exercises, labs, and observations evidenced.
- [ ] Pass threshold and all critical items independently verified.
- [ ] Attempts and remediation retained without overwrite.
- [ ] Role scope, restrictions, outstanding items, and access separation clear.
- [ ] Employee receives decision and approved review/appeal route.
- [ ] Record stored with minimum data and approved access.
- [ ] Refresher/re-certification date and change triggers recorded.
