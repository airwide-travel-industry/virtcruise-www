## Purpose

Use these controlled checklists for Finance decisions and periodic control evidence. A tick confirms that the operator performed the check against an authoritative source. It does not replace judgement, approval authority, or the audit record.

**Date:** ____________________  **Officer:** ____________________  **Supervisor:** ____________________

## 1. Daily Finance checklist

- [ ] Read Finance announcements, incidents, bank-account notices, and policy changes.
- [ ] Confirm production customer mode remains Manual Finance unless an approved activation notice exists.
- [ ] Confirm approved customer contact and payment-instruction routes are current.
- [ ] Review assigned, unassigned, overdue, and manual-intervention work through approved sources.
- [ ] Review proof scan failures, missing proof, integration exceptions, and notification failures.
- [ ] Review unknown and unallocated bank receipts in the approved reconciliation source.
- [ ] Review prior-shift handovers and replacement deadlines.
- [ ] Assign owners for priority exceptions.
- [ ] Confirm no policy-owned value required today remains unresolved.

## 2. Approval checklist

- [ ] Officer has authority under the approved authority schedule.
- [ ] Correct customer independently identified.
- [ ] Correct booking and invoice confirmed.
- [ ] Amount and currency match the case, bank record, and invoice context.
- [ ] Transfer reference matches the authoritative reference.
- [ ] Destination bank account is approved for the legal entity, currency, and effective date.
- [ ] Cleared funds independently verified in the approved bank/reconciliation source.
- [ ] Current proof belongs to the current review cycle.
- [ ] Current proof is accepted, clean, current, and not superseded.
- [ ] Proof details are consistent with, but not substituted for, cleared-funds verification.
- [ ] No duplicate Payment, allocation, proof, reference use, or decision found.
- [ ] Partial, full, underpayment, or overpayment treatment follows approved policy.
- [ ] No unresolved fraud, privacy, ownership, or sanctions/compliance concern under applicable policy.
- [ ] Required second approval obtained where the authority schedule requires it.
- [ ] Factual approval reason and cleared-funds attestation completed.
- [ ] Decision submitted once and authoritative result checked.
- [ ] Downstream Payment, allocation, Receipt, Ledger effect, booking milestone, and notifications monitored.

## 3. Rejection checklist

- [ ] Correct case, customer, booking, invoice, and current cycle confirmed.
- [ ] Rejection ground is factual and permitted by approved policy.
- [ ] Cleared-funds state has been checked where relevant.
- [ ] Internal reason contains no speculation or unnecessary sensitive data.
- [ ] Customer-safe reason states the correctable issue without internal investigation detail.
- [ ] Replacement eligibility checked separately.
- [ ] Terminal rejection chosen when replacement is not authorised or not lifecycle-eligible.
- [ ] Required supervisor/security escalation completed.
- [ ] Decision submitted once and final state confirmed.
- [ ] Customer communication is consistent with the authoritative state.

## 4. Replacement checklist

- [ ] Explicit Finance policy permits replacement for this reason.
- [ ] Case is eligible and has not been approved, expired, cancelled, financially completed, or otherwise closed.
- [ ] No previous replacement was used.
- [ ] Customer receives one replacement opportunity with the accepted 7-day deadline.
- [ ] Transfer, amount, currency, customer, booking, invoice, and reference remain unchanged.
- [ ] First review cycle remains immutable.
- [ ] Original proof is superseded and not used for the new decision.
- [ ] New proof is current, accepted, and clean.
- [ ] Review SLA restarts under the approved SLA policy.
- [ ] All approval or rejection checks are repeated.
- [ ] No Financial consequence exists before successful approval.

## 5. Fraud and suspicion checklist

- [ ] Pause approval, rejection, instruction change, or customer commitment as appropriate.
- [ ] Record observable facts only.
- [ ] Preserve current case, proof metadata, references, decisions, and timestamps.
- [ ] Check customer, payer, booking, invoice, amount, currency, account, and reference mismatches.
- [ ] Check duplicate/modified proof and repeated reference indicators.
- [ ] Check unknown payment and unusual urgency indicators.
- [ ] Do not accuse the customer or describe an investigation.
- [ ] Escalate through the approved Security/Finance route.
- [ ] Follow the authorised decision owner; do not investigate criminal conduct independently.
- [ ] Record customer communication boundary and next review time.

## 6. End-of-day checklist

- [ ] All assigned work is completed or handed over with a named owner.
- [ ] Overdue and unresolved high-risk cases are escalated.
- [ ] Ambiguous decisions and missing downstream records are documented.
- [ ] Unknown/unallocated payments and reconciliation breaks are handed over.
- [ ] Failed or uncertain notifications are assigned to an owner.
- [ ] Replacement deadlines and partial-payment balances are recorded for follow-up.
- [ ] No proof, financial report, or customer record remains on a personal device, local folder, ordinary mailbox, print tray, or desk.
- [ ] Approved handover record is complete and factual.
- [ ] Proof viewers and protected sessions are closed; operator has logged out.

## 7. Month-end checklist

- [ ] Approved month-end calendar and cut-off are confirmed.
- [ ] All bank accounts and currencies are reconciled to the authoritative Financial records.
- [ ] Payments, allocations, Receipts, refunds, and outstanding balances are reviewed.
- [ ] Unknown, unallocated, duplicate, partial, overpaid, and reversed items have named owners.
- [ ] Bank-account register and effective dates are reviewed; no real account details are copied into this checklist.
- [ ] Approval thresholds, exceptions, overrides, and segregation evidence are reviewed.
- [ ] Review cases approved near cut-off are traced to downstream Financial consequences.
- [ ] Booking confirmations are sampled against paid invoices and lawful milestone evaluation under approved audit policy.
- [ ] Notification failures with financial impact are reviewed.
- [ ] Ledger balance and suspense/exception positions are reviewed through the approved source.
- [ ] Retention, legal hold, and confidential disposal exceptions are reviewed.
- [ ] Material variances are assessed using approved thresholds.
- [ ] Supervisor/Manager sign-off and unresolved-item register are complete.
