## Purpose

Use these printable checks for consistent customer contacts. Record only necessary information in an approved support record. Never record passwords, verification or reset links, security codes, full bank details, or unnecessary identity documents.

## Call checklist

- [ ] Use the approved greeting and identify VirtCruise.
- [ ] Ask how you can help; do not interrupt the initial explanation.
- [ ] Verify identity proportionately before discussing protected information.
- [ ] Confirm a safe callback number only when required by approved policy.
- [ ] Record the booking, quote, invoice, payment, or receipt reference when relevant.
- [ ] Restate the issue and desired outcome.
- [ ] Check the authoritative customer-visible record; do not guess.
- [ ] Explain the next action, owner, and policy-approved expectation.
- [ ] Read back important references without exposing unrelated information.
- [ ] Summarise actions and close professionally.
- [ ] Complete the support record and escalation before ending the shift.

## Email checklist

- [ ] Confirm the recipient and customer context before opening protected records.
- [ ] Use the approved subject convention and greeting.
- [ ] State the answer or next action early.
- [ ] Use customer-friendly terms from DOC-002.
- [ ] Include only the minimum necessary customer information.
- [ ] Do not attach proof, internal notes, financial exports, or another customer's data.
- [ ] Do not send or repeat bank details; route payment instructions to Finance.
- [ ] Do not promise a booking or payment outcome.
- [ ] Check links, attachments, spelling, tone, and recipient before sending.
- [ ] Record the communication and any follow-up date in the approved system.

## Booking checklist

- [ ] Verify the customer before discussing a protected booking.
- [ ] Match the exact booking reference and customer account.
- [ ] Distinguish the quote, booking, payment, and notification status.
- [ ] Read the current booking status from the authoritative view.
- [ ] Check whether a customer action is available and appropriate.
- [ ] Explain that quote acceptance creates a booking but does not necessarily confirm travel.
- [ ] Do not promise availability, confirmation, cancellation outcome, or refund.
- [ ] Route commercial changes to the consultant or Operations.
- [ ] Route financial consequences to Finance.
- [ ] Record the advice and escalation reference.

## Payment enquiry checklist

- [ ] Verify identity and match the booking or invoice reference.
- [ ] Confirm the currency and amount the customer is asking about without requesting full bank details.
- [ ] Explain that Manual Finance is the current production mode.
- [ ] Direct the customer to the approved Finance route for bank instructions.
- [ ] Never invent, remember, repeat, or validate bank details.
- [ ] Never treat proof or a bank confirmation as cleared funds.
- [ ] Check the authoritative Financial Portal record available to the customer.
- [ ] If a Payment or Receipt is absent, transfer or escalate to Finance.
- [ ] Warn against a duplicate transfer while an earlier transfer is being checked.
- [ ] Do not confirm payment or booking; record the Finance hand-off.

## Complaint checklist

- [ ] Listen without arguing or assigning blame.
- [ ] Acknowledge the impact and show empathy without admitting unverified liability.
- [ ] Verify identity before discussing protected details.
- [ ] Separate facts, customer statements, and unresolved questions.
- [ ] Identify the requested resolution.
- [ ] Check records and prior contacts within your role.
- [ ] Classify urgency, vulnerability, financial risk, privacy risk, and travel proximity.
- [ ] Escalate to the accountable role when outside Support authority.
- [ ] Give the customer a case reference and policy-approved next update.
- [ ] Follow up, record the outcome, and close only when the resolution or hand-off is clear.

## Escalation checklist

- [ ] Protect the customer first; pause unsafe action.
- [ ] Select the correct owner in the [Support Escalation Matrix](SUPPORT-ESCALATION-MATRIX.md).
- [ ] Record the customer-safe reference, affected function, and observed impact.
- [ ] Record UTC date/time, channel, exact error wording, and steps already attempted.
- [ ] Remove passwords, tokens, full bank details, and unnecessary personal data.
- [ ] State what the customer was told and any promised follow-up.
- [ ] State urgency using approved policy; do not invent a severity or deadline.
- [ ] Use the approved internal route and obtain an escalation reference.
- [ ] Maintain ownership of customer communication unless formally handed over.
- [ ] Confirm closure and update the support record.
