## Purpose

Use this matrix to route an issue to the accountable role without exposing customer information or making decisions outside Support authority. Approved contacts, service levels, and opening hours belong in the restricted contact register; placeholders below require approval before operational publication.

## Escalation principles

1. Protect the customer and preserve accurate records.
2. Verify identity before discussing protected information.
3. Use the exact booking, quote, invoice, payment, receipt, or support reference.
4. Give the receiving role the minimum information needed.
5. Keep ownership of customer updates until the hand-off is acknowledged.
6. Never promise a resolution time unless the approved service policy permits it.

## Routing matrix

| Situation | First owner | Escalation owner | Support action | Do not do | Approved route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bank instructions or destination-account question | Finance Officer | Finance Supervisor | Transfer with invoice reference and currency | State or validate bank details | `[Finance route]` |
| Transfer not recorded or Receipt missing | Finance Officer | Finance Supervisor | Provide safe reference, currency, amount, and transfer time | Confirm payment or request another transfer | `[Finance route]` |
| Refund, cancellation charge, or financial dispute | Finance/Operations | Finance Manager/Operations Manager | Record the question and current booking state | Promise a refund or financial outcome | `[Finance/Operations route]` |
| Quote content, availability, or itinerary change | Consultant | Operations Manager | Transfer customer requirements and quote reference | Promise availability or price | `[Consultant route]` |
| Booking progression or operational exception | Operations | Operations Manager | Record current booking status and customer impact | Manually change status | `[Operations route]` |
| Registration, sign-in, or password-reset fault | Support | Administrator | Complete safe diagnosis and record exact message/time | Ask for password or reset link | `[Administrator route]` |
| Role or access problem | Administrator | Business Owner | Record requested function and current access result | Grant or promise permissions | `[Administrator route]` |
| Website unavailable, partial outage, or repeated error | Technical Operations | Operations Manager | Capture safe evidence and use incident wording | Diagnose infrastructure for the customer | `[Technical route]` |
| Notification delayed or uncertain | Operations/Technical Operations | Operations Manager | Check authoritative delivery state where authorised | Blindly resend or promise delivery | `[Notification route]` |
| Suspected account compromise, phishing, or malicious file | Security | Security/Privacy Lead | Pause action, preserve records, and escalate urgently | Investigate or forward suspicious content widely | `[Security route]` |
| Exposure of another customer's data | Security/Privacy | Security/Privacy Lead | Stop sharing, preserve facts, and report immediately | Copy, redistribute, or conceal the exposure | `[Privacy incident route]` |
| Serious complaint or unresolved service failure | Support Supervisor | Management | Own communication and provide a concise case history | Blame staff or promise compensation | `[Management route]` |
| Immediate traveller welfare concern | Operations Manager | Management/Security as policy directs | Follow approved emergency and traveller-assistance policy | Improvise emergency advice outside training | `[Emergency route]` |

## Priority guidance

Exact response times and severity definitions are policy-owned. Apply the approved Support Operations Policy. In the absence of an approved value, escalate rather than inventing one.

| Indicator | Handling principle |
|---|---|
| Active privacy or security exposure | Stop further exposure and use the approved urgent route |
| Travel in progress or imminent | Flag travel proximity and customer impact accurately |
| Financial loss may increase | Pause unsafe action and involve Finance or Security |
| Service affects several customers | Notify the incident owner; do not create separate technical diagnoses |
| Routine question with no immediate risk | Use the normal queue and communicate the next action |

## Escalation record

Record:

- customer-safe support reference;
- customer identity verification outcome, not secret answers;
- affected booking or business reference;
- UTC date and time;
- observed symptom and exact customer-visible message;
- impact and travel proximity;
- safe troubleshooting completed;
- current authoritative state;
- what the customer was told;
- receiving role and escalation reference; and
- next communication owner.
