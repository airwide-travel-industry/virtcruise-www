## Purpose

Create your account, explore travel, request a quote, follow a booking, and get help safely in your first session.

## Who should use this guide

New VirtCruise customers using their own records. Allow about 30–45 minutes. You need access to your email and the details VirtCruise asked you to provide.

> **Important:** This guide is a desk reference, not the full procedure. It does not replace DOC-002 through DOC-007, DOC-009, or DOC-010. Status meanings always come from DOC-007.

## Daily workflow

```text
Sign in → Check messages/status → Complete one eligible action
→ Verify the result → Sign out on shared devices
```

## Top 10 tasks

1. **Create an account.** Open **Create account**, enter your own accurate details, accept required terms, submit once, and note the check-your-email message.
2. **Verify email.** Open the newest VirtCruise verification message and use its link once. If it expired, request a new message rather than reusing it.
3. **Log in.** Use your email and password. Never share a password or verification/reset link, including with Support.
4. **Browse packages.** Compare destination, duration, inclusions, exclusions, pricing basis, terms, and availability wording. Public content may change until booking is confirmed.
5. **Request a quote.** Add the required services, travellers, dates, contact details, and requests; review the summary; submit once; retain the quote reference.
6. **Review and accept a quote.** Open **My Quotes**, check the current offer and terms, and use **Accept quote** only when available and correct. Acceptance does not itself confirm travel.
7. **Follow your booking.** Open **My Bookings**, use the booking reference, and read the current status. Use [DOC-007 customer-visible statuses](../reference/STATUS-LIFECYCLE-REFERENCE.md#21-customer-visible-statuses) rather than guessing.
8. **Use Manual Finance.** Follow only the approved Finance contact instructions displayed for your currency and quote/invoice reference. Production Manual Finance does not provide self-service bank details or proof upload. A transfer is not confirmed until Finance verifies cleared funds.
9. **Use the Customer Portal.** Review your own quotes, bookings, finances, trips, profile, and notifications. A dashboard summary does not replace the detailed record.
10. **Get support.** Use the approved **Contact** or Support route. Give the relevant quote, booking, invoice, Payment, Receipt, or support reference—never a password, security code, full payment data, or proof file unless an approved secure flow asks for it.

## Quick checklist

### Start-of-day checklist

- [ ] Use a trusted device/network and the genuine VirtCruise address.
- [ ] Sign in to your own account and check the current detailed record.
- [ ] Keep the correct quote or booking reference ready.

### Top task checklist

- [ ] Confirm you selected your own record.
- [ ] Read the current status and required action.
- [ ] Review dates, travellers, currency, amounts, and terms before submitting.
- [ ] Submit once, wait for the result, and save the customer-safe reference.
- [ ] Refresh the authoritative record before repeating an uncertain action.

### End-of-day checklist

- [ ] Confirm your requested action appears in the relevant detailed record.
- [ ] Record any Support reference and next expected update.
- [ ] Sign out, especially on a shared device; close sensitive messages/documents.

## Common mistakes

- Treating quote acceptance, an uploaded proof, a transfer, or a Receipt as Booking Confirmation.
- Reading a notification or dashboard card instead of the authoritative booking/Financial record.
- Submitting a quote or transfer twice when the first result is uncertain.
- Sending proof or sensitive data through ordinary email or messaging without an approved request.

## Do's and don'ts

### Things you should always do

- Keep references and compare currency, amount, dates, travellers, and outstanding balance.
- Contact VirtCruise when a status conflicts with a direct communication.
- Report suspected account compromise immediately through the approved Support route.

### Things you must never do

- Share passwords, one-time codes, reset links, or another person's records.
- Use bank details from an old message or third party; Manual Finance supplies approved contact guidance, not self-service bank instructions.
- Assume `DEPOSIT_PENDING` means a booking is confirmed or that Finance lost your message.

## Escalation

| Issue | Owner | Escalate when | Do not escalate when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quote, itinerary, availability | Consultant | Current quote is unclear, wrong, or cannot be accepted | You have not yet read the current quote/terms |
| Transfer, balance, Receipt | Finance | Reference is correct but authoritative Financial record is missing/wrong | You only just submitted and the stated processing step remains pending |
| Booking progression | Operations | Financial record and booking status conflict or travel is affected | The displayed status already explains the next action |
| Sign-in/reset | Support | Newest valid flow fails after one careful retry | A field message identifies a correction you have not made |
| Security/privacy | Security/Privacy via Support | Immediately for suspected compromise or another customer's data | Never investigate or forward suspicious content yourself |

Support owns the customer conversation until handoff is acknowledged. Provide the minimum safe facts and what you observed; do not diagnose internal systems.
