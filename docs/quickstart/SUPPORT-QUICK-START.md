## Purpose

Verify customers, answer common questions, support account recovery, and route financial, operational, incident, privacy, and security issues safely.

## Who should use this guide

New Support team members using approved support tools. No distinct released Support product role is established by v0.7.0 evidence; use only separately approved access.

> **Important:** This guide does not replace DOC-002 through DOC-007, DOC-009, or DOC-010. It does not grant record access or authority.

## Daily workflow

```text
Review queue/incidents → Verify customer → Identify record and lifecycle
→ Resolve safe question or route → Confirm owner/customer update → Handover
```

## Top 10 tasks

1. **Review active work.** Check assigned cases, acknowledged escalations, travel-imminent customers, known incidents, and approved service messages.
2. **Verify the customer.** Use the approved verification level for the requested information/action. Record pass/fail, never secret answers.
3. **Identify the issue.** Separate account, quote, booking, bank-transfer review, Payment, Receipt, notification, content, complaint, privacy, and security concerns.
4. **Use the exact reference.** Match the customer-owned quote, booking, invoice, Payment, Receipt, or support reference before discussing detail.
5. **Answer common questions.** Explain navigation and customer-visible meaning from DOC-002 and DOC-007. Do not reinterpret state or promise an outcome.
6. **Support password reset.** Direct the customer to **Forgot password**, newest valid email, and one-time link. Support never asks for a password, code, or reset link and never sets a password for them.
7. **Explain Manual Finance.** Point to the approved displayed Finance contact route/reference and unpaid/unconfirmed wording. Do not provide or validate bank details.
8. **Handle an application problem.** Capture UTC time, route/action, exact safe message, request/support reference, scope, customer impact, and one safe reproduction result; check known incidents.
9. **Escalate correctly.** Send the minimum facts to the accountable owner and retain customer communication until acknowledgement.
10. **Close or hand over.** Record verification outcome, facts, action, current authoritative state, customer wording, owner/reference, and next communication point.

## Quick checklist

### Start-of-day checklist

- [ ] Confirm approved Support access, routes, current incidents, and escalation contacts.
- [ ] Review assigned/open cases and high-impact/travel-imminent work.
- [ ] Open DOC-005 checklists/escalation matrix and DOC-007 status reference.

### Top task checklist

- [ ] Verify identity and ownership at the required level.
- [ ] Match the exact business reference and lifecycle.
- [ ] Read the authoritative detailed record; use safe customer wording.
- [ ] Complete only approved troubleshooting; avoid duplicate actions.
- [ ] Resolve or obtain an acknowledged owner and record the next update.

### End-of-day checklist

- [ ] Update customers whose approved communication point is due.
- [ ] Handover unresolved financial, travel, incident, privacy, security, or access risks.
- [ ] Remove unsafe notes/downloads, close records, and sign out.

## Common mistakes

- Discussing protected details before verification or using excessive personal data.
- Treating a customer report, dashboard, email, or uploaded proof as authoritative state.
- Blindly resending notifications or repeating quote/payment/review actions.
- Diagnosing infrastructure, fraud, or staff fault to the customer.

## Do's and don'ts

### Things you should always do

- Show empathy, state verified facts, give the next owner/action, and maintain a clear case history.
- Check for known incidents and related duplicate cases before escalating.
- Escalate privacy/security exposure immediately and minimise further disclosure.

### Things you must never do

- Ask for passwords, one-time codes, reset links, full payment details, or unnecessary proof.
- Promise availability, cleared funds, refund, booking confirmation, delivery, compensation, or resolution time without authority.
- Expose internal comments, reviewer identity, audit, storage, notification message bodies, or another customer's information.

## Escalation

| Issue | Owner | Escalate when | Do not escalate when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quote/availability | Consultant | Current quote or itinerary needs business input | DOC-002/current quote already answers it |
| Transfer/Payment/Receipt/refund | Finance | Verification, mismatch, missing record, or decision required | Customer only needs the approved Finance route |
| Booking/travel/cancellation | Operations | Progression conflict, fulfilment risk, or travel impact | Current state/action is clear and expected |
| Access/role | Administrator | Valid recovery fails or approved role access differs | User has not completed safe standard steps |
| Outage/repeated fault/notification | Technical Operations | Multi-user impact, known-service symptoms, or authoritative delivery failure | Isolated correctable input error |
| Privacy/security | Security/Privacy | Immediately for exposure, compromise, phishing, or malicious content | Never perform an amateur investigation |

DOC-003 owns operational escalation and DOC-004 owns Finance decisions. Do not escalate when a safe documented answer resolves the issue and no risk or ownership transfer remains.
