## Purpose

Prepare complete package drafts and move immutable versions through independent review and controlled publication.

## Who should use this guide

Content Editors and, where explicitly noted, independent Approvers. The Content Studio interface is planned; use this guide with the accepted service/workflow and DOC-006, not as evidence that an unreleased screen exists.

> **Important:** This guide does not replace DOC-002 through DOC-007, DOC-009, or DOC-010. DOC-007 owns lifecycle meaning; only `DRAFT` is editable.

## Daily workflow

```text
Check assigned packages → Edit one DRAFT → Preview/check
→ Submit immutable version → Independent review → Schedule/publish
→ Verify public projection and record handover
```

## Top 10 tasks

1. **Confirm package and version.** Match business code, version number, source version, owner, and current DOC-007 state before editing.
2. **Create or derive a draft.** Start a new `DRAFT` from the approved source. Restoration means deriving a new numbered draft, not reopening a retired version.
3. **Edit structured content.** Keep title, summary, description, destination, duration, highlights, inclusions, exclusions, itinerary, terms, frequently asked questions, and call to action consistent.
4. **Check pricing.** Confirm currency, amount or price-on-request, basis, qualifier, validity, selection, and order against approved commercial input. Never invent price or availability.
5. **Check media.** Verify allowed reference/type, rights, privacy, role/order, meaningful alternative text, and customer-safe content. Do not upload unlicensed or sensitive media.
6. **Check search engine optimisation (SEO).** Use the accepted metadata fields only; write accurate human-readable copy and avoid unsupported schema or misleading claims.
7. **Preview.** Inspect structured content, pricing, media, accessibility, responsive presentation, and customer safety together using fictional/test-safe data where applicable.
8. **Submit for review.** Save the latest draft, resolve conflicts, complete the publishing checklist, and submit. `IN_REVIEW` is sealed; it is not editable.
9. **Review and approve/reject.** An independent Approver checks content, commercial facts, media, accessibility, SEO, and publication readiness, then records a reasoned decision. Rejection requires a new derived draft.
10. **Schedule/publish/retire.** An Approver verifies effective pricing and clean public media, then publishes now or schedules an unambiguous future time. Verify the public projection. Retirement removes it while retaining history.

## Quick checklist

### Start-of-day checklist

- [ ] Confirm Editor/Approver role, assignments, approved commercial inputs, and media rights.
- [ ] Identify current published version and any active draft/review/schedule.
- [ ] Open DOC-006 publishing checklist and DOC-007 content lifecycle.

### Top task checklist

- [ ] Edit only the current owned `DRAFT` and resolve version conflicts safely.
- [ ] Check content, pricing, media, SEO, accessibility, and preview.
- [ ] Submit once and confirm immutable `IN_REVIEW`.
- [ ] Keep review independent and reasoned.
- [ ] Verify schedule/public projection or retirement outcome.

### End-of-day checklist

- [ ] Save eligible drafts and record their version/next owner.
- [ ] Hand over pending reviews, schedules, rights issues, and publication exceptions.
- [ ] Confirm no unapproved media/local customer data remains open or stored.

## Common mistakes

- Editing or “correcting” an `IN_REVIEW`, `APPROVED`, `PUBLISHED`, `REJECTED`, or `RETIRED` version.
- Treating Restored as a status rather than a new-draft derivation.
- Publishing a valid layout with unapproved price, rights, accessibility, or effective time.
- Assuming planned discard or schedule-cancellation controls are available.

## Do's and don'ts

### Things you should always do

- Preserve versions, source relationships, reasons, and independent approval.
- Preview at Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile sizes and provide meaningful alternative text.
- Verify the effective public result after publish/schedule/retire.

### Things you must never do

- Bypass review, self-approve where independence is required, or change state directly.
- Reuse rejected/retired versions in place or overwrite another editor's work.
- Publish personal data, unlicensed media, unsupported HTML/schema, or unverified commercial claims.

## Escalation

| Issue | Owner | Escalate when | Do not escalate when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Content/commercial ambiguity | Editorial/Commercial owner | Source facts conflict or approval is missing | A current approved source answers it |
| Version conflict | Content Lead | Refresh/compare cannot safely reconcile intent | You can reapply your intended edit to current draft |
| Rights/privacy/accessibility | Media or Privacy/Accessibility owner | Rights, consent, exposure, or equivalent text is uncertain | The approved asset record and checklist are complete |
| Schedule/public projection | Publisher/Operations | Effective time/result is wrong or uncertain | Scheduled time has not yet arrived |
| Service/permission fault | Administrator/Technical Operations | Accepted workflow or authorised access fails | The action is ineligible in current DOC-007 state |

DOC-003 coordinates operational publication exceptions; Finance (DOC-004) owns financial records, not catalogue price copy approval.
