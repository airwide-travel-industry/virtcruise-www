## Purpose

Choose the shortest guide for your role and complete safe first-hour tasks. These printable desk references point to the detailed manuals for policy, exceptions, and full procedures.

> **Important:** These guides do not replace DOC-002, DOC-003, DOC-004, DOC-005, DOC-006, DOC-007, DOC-009, or DOC-010. They do not grant access, authority, or permission. The application, approved policy, and authoritative state remain controlling.

## First-hour route

1. Read the purpose, boundaries, and start-of-day checklist for your role.
2. Confirm your approved account and permissions; a guide does not provide either.
3. Complete only tasks whose controls are visible and whose current state is eligible.
4. Keep the relevant detailed manual open for exceptions.
5. Use DOC-007 for every lifecycle meaning; do not interpret or rename a status.
6. Practise escalation using fictional references before handling live work.
7. Finish the end-of-day checklist before handover or sign-out.

## Pack-wide safety rules

### Always

- Verify identity, ownership, current authoritative state, and permission before action.
- Use exact, customer-safe references and minimum necessary information.
- Refresh after conflict or uncertainty and record a safe handover.
- Use the owning manual for the full procedure and DOC-007 for status meaning.

### Never

- Change state directly, bypass workflow, or act from a stale screen.
- Promise price, availability, cleared funds, booking confirmation, delivery, or resolution without authoritative evidence.
- Expose credentials, proof files, internal comments, reviewer identity, audit data, or another customer's record.
- Invent a contact, service level, policy, lifecycle state, or unsupported control.
