## Purpose

Handle customer, quote, and booking enquiries accurately while staying within accepted Consultant access and escalation boundaries.

## Who should use this guide

New Consultants with approved tools and policy. The released evidence does not establish a Consultant back-office mutation/search screen and denies Finance Operations Portal access; use only separately approved Consultant sources.

> **Important:** This guide does not grant access and does not replace DOC-002 through DOC-007, DOC-009, or DOC-010. Do not redefine a DOC-007 state.

## Daily workflow

```text
Open assigned work → Verify customer and reference → Read authoritative record
→ Explain or route → Record outcome/owner → Secure handover
```

## Top 10 tasks

1. **Open assigned work.** Use only the approved Consultant queue/source; never search through a role or portal you are not authorised to use.
2. **Verify the customer.** Follow approved verification before discussing protected quote or booking detail. Record only the result, never secret answers.
3. **Look up a customer.** Use the approved customer reference and minimum necessary data. If no accepted lookup source is available, escalate—do not improvise.
4. **Look up a booking.** Use the exact booking reference in the approved source and distinguish booking status from quote, payment, review, or notification status.
5. **Support a quote.** Confirm the current quote, dates, travellers, services, customer-visible notes, value, currency, and eligibility. Do not promise availability or final confirmation.
6. **Explain acceptance.** Tell the customer that an eligible quote can be accepted from their owned view; acceptance begins booking progression but is not Booking Confirmation.
7. **Communicate clearly.** State what is known, current customer-safe status, action owner, next step, and any policy-approved expectation. Use DOC-007 wording.
8. **Route Finance questions.** Send bank instructions, transfer verification, Payment, Receipt, balance, or refund matters to Finance with safe references. Consultants cannot enter Finance operations.
9. **Route operational issues.** Send booking progression, travel readiness, service fulfilment, or cancellation-policy issues to Operations with customer impact and travel proximity.
10. **Close or hand over.** Record observation, action, receiving owner/reference, and what the customer was told. Keep communication ownership until handoff is acknowledged.

## Quick checklist

### Start-of-day checklist

- [ ] Confirm approved Consultant access and current communication routes.
- [ ] Review assigned work, travel-imminent cases, and acknowledged handovers.
- [ ] Open DOC-005 for communication/escalation and DOC-007 for status meaning.

### Top task checklist

- [ ] Verify customer identity and record ownership.
- [ ] Match the exact quote/booking reference.
- [ ] Read the authoritative detailed record and current state.
- [ ] Answer only within Consultant knowledge and authority.
- [ ] Record a concise outcome or acknowledged escalation.

### End-of-day checklist

- [ ] Update open customer communications with approved expectations.
- [ ] Hand over travel-imminent, financial, privacy, security, or incident risks.
- [ ] Close customer records, clear unsafe notes/downloads, and sign out.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming Consultant status grants customer search, mutation, or Finance access.
- Calling a quote accepted, an invoice paid, or a message sent “booking confirmed.”
- Copying internal comments or speculative diagnoses into customer communication.
- Promising price, availability, refund, response time, or replacement proof eligibility.

## Do's and don'ts

### Things you should always do

- Verify, use exact references, and separate each lifecycle.
- Use factual, customer-safe language and preserve the communication trail.
- Escalate access gaps instead of borrowing credentials or another role's screen.

### Things you must never do

- Access Finance proof, review, Payment, Receipt, Ledger, or internal notification data.
- Change booking or lifecycle state directly or tell another team to bypass workflow.
- Ask for passwords, codes, reset links, unnecessary financial data, or proof through ordinary messaging.

## Escalation

| Issue | Owner | Escalate when | Do not escalate when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quote content/availability | Consultant lead / Operations | Current approved source cannot resolve it or customer impact is material | The current quote answers the question |
| Payment/Receipt/bank transfer | Finance | Any verification, balance, allocation, receipt, refund, or bank-detail decision is needed | The customer only needs the approved Finance route |
| Booking progression/travel | Operations | States conflict, fulfilment is blocked, or travel is near/in progress | The authoritative booking already shows the expected next step |
| Account access | Support/Administrator | Safe customer steps fail or role access is incorrect | User has not followed the current valid flow |
| Privacy/security | Security/Privacy | Immediately on suspected compromise, malicious content, or data exposure | Never investigate beyond safe preservation |

Use DOC-003 for operational ownership and DOC-004 for Finance boundaries. Do not escalate routine questions without first checking the current approved source.
